Platform · Five Applications
MYZYK
Africa's creative rights and royalty platform
Myzyk is the operating system of African music rights. It registers every creative contributor, tracks every play across every venue on the network, calculates royalties in real time and distributes direct payments to every rights holder on the last banking day of every month. No minimum threshold. No opacity. No exclusions.
  • AI-powered rights registry — audio and video assets, 10+ contributor categories including session musicians, choreographers and dancers
  • Real-time play logging — every patron request, DJ/VJ session and playlist play attributed and calculated instantly
  • Direct mobile money distribution — M-Pesa, MTN MoMo — last banking day of every month
  • Watch & Listen Party — live network-wide music debut events for premium artists
  • Joint Party — multi-city live DJ/VJ broadcast with voice-activated city switching
  • African Cinema Network — 250,000 screens activated for African film by Year 3
Hardware · Three Tiers
ZYK UNIT
The physical portal in every room on the network
The ZYK Unit is a self-reliant networked audio and video performance terminal. It arrives at a venue pre-configured, pre-paired and pre-connected. The venue owner provides a wall socket. The unit does the rest — playing music, logging every play, attributing every royalty and keeping the Joint Party alive regardless of what the grid, the generator or the satellite decide to do.
  • Solar panel + LiFePO4 power wall — 8–10 hours full operation independent of the grid
  • Starlink primary connectivity via Zoshani enterprise agreement — pan-African satellite broadband
  • Dual SIM 4G Zoshani-managed failover — automatic switching, zero venue owner involvement
  • Concert-quality audio, cinema-quality video — calibrated per room, per tier
  • Voice command city switching — DJMYZYK Joint Party in under 1 second
  • Three tiers: ZYK Corner · ZYK Lounge · ZYK Arena — 100m² to 300m²+
